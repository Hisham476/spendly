Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create an account, we collect your name, email address and password (stored in hashed form). When you use Spendly, we also store the expense records you enter, such as amount, category, date and description.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your information only to run the service: signing you in, showing your expenses and summaries, and keeping your account secure. We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored in the Spendly database. We take reasonable measures to protect it from unauthorised access, but no system is completely secure, so please keep your password private. You can ask us to delete your account and the expense records associated with it.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may log basic technical details such as your IP address when the font is requested. We do not share your expense data with any other third-party service.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or want to exercise your data rights, please contact the Spendly team.
